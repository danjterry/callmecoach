About Greg Taylor

Part coach, part catalyst — all in on your people.

Greg Taylor
Executive Coach & Keynote Speaker

For more than 30 years — through the wins, the knocks, and everything in between — Greg Taylor has helped leaders and organizations do the hard, human work of personal transformation. Using a process of insight and discovery he's refined across decades in corporate and personal leadership, he helps people learn from and inspire themselves, then carry that change to everyone around them.

He's not chasing some idealized vision of perfection. He's after steady progress — the daily, incremental improvements that compound over time into complete transformation. On stage that looks like high energy and hard-won stories; in the weeks after, it looks like teams who've taken another step, and another, toward the results they want most. Greg judges his own success by his clients'.

He's the author of Find Your Winning Edge and host of The Leadership Factory podcast — and his coaching practice was named one of the Top 20 Leadership Development & Coaching Companies of 2022.

Planner FAQs

How is pricing determined?

Every engagement is custom. After a short discovery call we factor in audience size, format, length, travel, and prep — then send a tailored quote. No preset pricing.

How far in advance should we book?

Six weeks to three months is ideal. The sooner you lock a date, the sooner we start tailoring the talk to your audience and goals.

Will Greg tailor the talk to us?

Always. Each keynote is built around your industry, your language, and the specific outcome you want people to walk away with.

Do you offer follow-up after the event?

Yes — post-event webinars, one-on-one coaching, and online learning to turn the energy in the room into lasting change.

In-person or virtual?

Both. Greg loves the energy of a live room, but delivers high-impact virtual keynotes and workshops too.

What audiences does Greg speak to?

Corporate teams, conferences, associations, school boards and districts, and leadership offsites — anywhere people need to align and perform.
